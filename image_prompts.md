# Image Generation Prompts for TAF College Website

This document contains text-to-image generation prompts tailored for the TAF College website project. The prompts are designed to match the specific context (headers, course features, team members, etc.) found in the codebase.

## Global & Branding

**File:** `img/tafcollege-logo.png`
**Context:** Main Logo (Header/Footer)
**Prompt:** A modern, professional logo for "TAF College". The design should be sleek and academic yet creative, incorporating elements of arts and technology. Minimalist vector style, bold typography, deep blue and white color scheme.

**File:** `img/carousel-1.jpg`
**Context:** Main Hero Background (Home Page)
**Prompt:** A wide, high-resolution cinematic shot of a diverse group of college students collaborating in a modern, sunlit creative campus hub. Students are working on fashion designs, music mixing consoles, and video editing stations. The atmosphere is energetic and inspiring. Professional photography, shallow depth of field, 16:9 aspect ratio.

**File:** `img/carousel-2.jpg`
**Context:** Secondary Page Header Background
**Prompt:** An architectural wide shot of a futuristic and stylish college building interior, featuring glass walls, open collaborative spaces, and greenery. Bright, airy, and welcoming. Professional architectural photography, 16:9 aspect ratio.
