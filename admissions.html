Admissions

Your Path to TAF College

We welcome learners who are ready to build practical, job-ready skills. Our admissions process is simple, transparent, and designed to help you choose the right program.

Intakes: Jan, May, Sep
Duration: 6-18 months
Modes: Full-time or weekend

Admissions at a glance

  • Online application form
  • Document review
  • Short interview (if required)
  • Offer letter within 7 days
  • Enrollment support
How to Apply

Five Simple Steps

1
Submit your application

Complete the online form and select your preferred program and study mode.

2
Upload required documents

Provide academic results, ID, and any portfolio work if applicable.

3
Admissions review

Our team reviews your application and may schedule a short interview.

4
Receive your offer

Successful applicants receive an offer letter with next steps.

5
Enroll and begin classes

Confirm enrollment, pay fees, and attend orientation.

Entry Requirements

Who Can Apply

WASSCE/SSSCE

Minimum passes in core subjects and relevant electives depending on program.

Mature Applicants

Applicants aged 25+ with work experience may take an entry assessment.

Transfer Students

Credits may be transferred with transcripts and curriculum evaluation.
