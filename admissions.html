How to Apply

Six Simple Steps

1
Submit your application

Complete the TAF College online application form with your contact details, chosen programme, and a short statement explaining why you want to study at TAF College.

2
Application review

Every application is reviewed within 5 working days. We assess motivation, learning readiness, and entrepreneurial ambition, not grades alone.

3
Admissions interview

Attend a 15-minute conversation in person or by WhatsApp voice note so we can understand your goals and readiness.

4
Offer letter issued

Successful applicants receive a digital offer letter within 3 working days of their interview, confirming programme, cohort, and fee or scholarship status.

5
Confirm your place

Pay your registration deposit as a fee-paying student or sign your Julian Opuni Foundation Scholarship Agreement as a scholarship student.

6
Attend orientation and begin

Attend orientation on 24-25 September 2026. Lectures begin on 28 September 2026.

Entry Requirements

Who Can Apply

No Grade Barrier

No WASSCE, A-level, or specific grade requirement. Motivation and potential are the primary criteria.

Age and Background

Applicants should generally be 17+ and have completed secondary school or equivalent. Younger applicants may be considered case by case.

Readiness to Learn

Working English competency is required. Applicants with prior work experience or self-taught skills are strongly encouraged.
