Programs Built for Careers

Each programme blends practical training, studio work, industry mentorship, and live venture experience. All core programmes run for 6 months: 5 months of teaching followed by 1 month of internship or capstone project.

Assessment is 70% practical. Every student graduates with a professional portfolio, a real client or business track record, and a TAF College Certificate.
