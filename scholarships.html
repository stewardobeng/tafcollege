Julian Opuni Scholars Programme

Full Tuition-Free Places for Ghana's Most Ambitious Young People

This is not a conventional scholarship or bursary programme. It is a comprehensive workforce and enterprise development initiative designed to identify high-potential young Ghanaians, equip them with globally relevant skills, and transition them into sustainable income generation, freelancing careers, and entrepreneurial ventures.

Scholarship Overview

What the Programme Covers

Full Tuition Waiver

The full programme tuition is covered from acceptance through graduation.

Industry Mentorship

Each scholar is paired with a senior professional from their chosen sector for weekly support.

Foundation Network

Scholars gain access to the Julian Opuni Foundation alumni network in Ghana and the diaspora.

Incubation Priority

First access to venture incubation resources, startup support, and seed funding opportunities from Month 4 onwards.

Industry Exposure

Workshops, site visits, and networking events funded by the Foundation throughout the programme.

Post-Graduation Support

Active employment, freelance, and placement support for 12 months after completion.

Selection Criteria

What We Look For

01
Entrepreneurial Mindset

How the applicant thinks about problems, opportunities, business, and practical solutions.

02
Digital Aptitude

Learning agility and ability to adapt quickly to new digital and technical skills.

03
Motivation and Resilience

Evidence of personal drive, discipline, growth potential, and readiness to work hard.

04
Leadership Potential

Community contribution, peer influence, initiative, and willingness to give back.

05
Financial Need

Genuine financial barrier to participation without full scholarship support.

06
Standard Admission

Scholarship applicants must also complete the standard TAF College admissions application.

How to Apply

Scholarship Timeline

Scholarship applications open on 1 June 2026. Places are limited to 20 full tuition-free awards and applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.

Apply for admission first, then complete the scholarship pathway when requested by the admissions team.
