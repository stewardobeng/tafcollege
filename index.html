Our Programs

Fashion Design

From foundational sketching, colour theory, and fabric knowledge through to advanced garment construction, pattern making, and a final 3-5 piece collection showcase. Students operate the TAF Fashion Label, producing corporate wear, school uniforms, and ready-to-wear collections for paying clients.

Skills: Garment construction, pattern making, illustration, collections, branding, sustainability, client management.
Duration: 6 months | 3 days/week | 4 hours/day.
Career outcomes: Fashion designer, brand owner, garment entrepreneur, export business.

Read More

Digital Marketing

Content creation, social media strategy, paid advertising, SEO, email marketing, and client acquisition. Students run real campaigns for real clients through the TAF Digital & AI Agency, with Month 5 focused on freelancing, personal branding, and securing income before graduation.

Skills: Content strategy, Meta Ads, Google Ads, SEO, email marketing, analytics, freelancing.
Duration: 6 months | 3 days/week | 4 hours/day.
Career outcomes: Marketing consultant, agency founder, freelancer, in-house digital marketer.

Read More

Applied AI

AI literacy, prompt engineering, generative content creation, AI for productivity, and vibe coding. Students learn to use AI tools confidently, ethically, and commercially, then complete a capstone applying the full AI workflow to a real business or community problem.

Skills: Prompt engineering, ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini, automation, Canva AI, vibe coding, AI workflow design.
Duration: 6 months | 3 days/week | 4 hours/day.
Career outcomes: AI consultant, productivity specialist, tech entrepreneur, freelancer.

Read More

Photo and Video Production

Camera handling, portrait and event photography, lighting and audio production, commercial video, social media content, and professional editing. Students operate the TAF Media & Production Agency, serving paying clients across Accra.

Skills: Photography, videography, Adobe Premiere, Lightroom, DaVinci Resolve, lighting, audio, live production.
Duration: 6 months | 3 days/week | 4 hours/day.
Career outcomes: Photographer, videographer, content creator, media agency founder.
