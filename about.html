Vision, Mission & Values

Our Vision

To be the premier institution for creative and digital skills in West Africa, fostering a generation of innovators and leaders who drive economic growth.

Our Mission

To empower unemployed youth through practical, industry-relevant training in digital media, fashion, and beauty, equipping them with the tools to succeed as entrepreneurs and professionals.

Core Values

Innovation: embracing new ideas.
Excellence: striving for the best.
Integrity: acting with honesty.

Meet Our Visionary Founder

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world."

Johnie Ocquaye established TAF College with a singular mission: to bridge the gap between talent and opportunity in Africa. With over 20 years of experience in education and youth development, he saw the immense potential in Ghana's youth and the need for practical, industry-aligned training.

His vision is to create a hub of creativity and innovation where young minds are not only taught skills but are mentored to become leaders and entrepreneurs. Under his leadership, TAF College has grown into a premier institution, transforming hundreds of lives and contributing significantly to the creative economy.
