Who We Are

Practical Skills for Real Economic Outcomes

TAF College is a practical skills, entrepreneurship, and innovation institution based in Dzorwulu, Accra. We serve young professionals, aspiring entrepreneurs, recent school leavers, and people who have been excluded from traditional university pathways but want a real, market-ready qualification.

Every programme is built around one question: what does this student need to know, make, or do to earn a living and build a business? That question shapes the curriculum, assessments, mentorship, and live commercial ventures students operate while they study.

A Production Environment, Not Just a Classroom

TAF College is not a theory institution. It is a production environment. Students are expected to produce, create, sell, and build from their first week. By graduation, they have a portfolio of real work, a network of real contacts, and the practical confidence of someone who has already done the work.

Founding Story

Built to Convert Ambition Into Economic Reality

TAF College was founded on the conviction that Africa's youth employment crisis is not caused by a lack of ambition or intelligence, but by a lack of systems capable of converting ambition into economic reality.

Ghana, like much of West Africa, faces a structural mismatch between what young people are taught and what the economy rewards. TAF College was designed as a direct response: a purpose-built institution combining vocational skills, entrepreneurship education, AI and digital fluency, mentorship, and real commercial experience within a focused programme model.

Vision, Mission & Values

Our Vision

TAF College envisions an Africa where young people are empowered not merely as job seekers, but as entrepreneurs, innovators, creators, technical professionals, problem-solvers, and builders of sustainable economic systems.

Our Mission

TAF College's mission is to provide accessible, practical, and future-focused education that equips underserved young Africans with the skills, mindset, mentorship, and opportunities needed to achieve sustainable economic participation and entrepreneurial success.

Meet Our Visionary Founder

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world."

Johnnie Oquaye established TAF College with a singular mission: to bridge the gap between talent and opportunity in Africa. With over 20 years of experience in education and youth development, he saw the immense potential in Ghana's youth and the need for practical, industry-aligned training.

His vision is to create a hub of creativity and innovation where young minds are not only taught skills but are mentored to become leaders and entrepreneurs. Under his leadership, TAF College has grown into a premier institution, transforming hundreds of lives and contributing significantly to the creative economy.
